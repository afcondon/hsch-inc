### prim-zoo-mosh untangled

`psd3-prim-zoo-mosh` was two unrelated things:

- **The Morphism Zoo** — recursion schemes, children's-book /
  anatomical-museum aesthetic. Self-contained static bundle, only needs
  `hylograph-graph`. Wants its own site (`zoo.hylograph.net`).
- **An L-system library** (`Zoo.LSystem.*`) that the blog consumed and
  that commit `68517ae` deleted — the commit where the children's-book
  version *replaced* the earlier hylograph-drawn attempt.

**The blog had been broken since that commit.** `blog/output/` held 996
modules from a 20 July build but no `Zoo.*` and no
`Hylographic.Viz.LSystemPlant`; the last good bundle was 5 February.

Resolved by cutting: deleted `src/Viz/LSystemPlant.purs` and its wiring
from `src/Main.purs` (import, `plantGeneration` state field, slot, proxy,
render slot, the two `RouteChanged` branches — which collapse to a single
`fromMaybe NotFound`) and `src/Viz/Registry.purs`. **The blog now builds
for the first time since February.**

Also fixed `psd3-prim-zoo-mosh/spago.yaml`, which pinned
`hylograph-graph` to an absolute `/Users/afc/...` path.

## Decisions Made

- **Videos, not live demos, for the public site.** Showcases that need a
  backend become videos rather than requiring a running fleet. A series
  extends by appending; a fleet extends by adding services.
- **YouTube as the host**, same as Kibitzer. (R2 was floated on
  digital-sovereignty grounds and set aside in favour of consistency with
  existing practice.)
- **The Docker fleet stays** — as the Docker-deployment test bed and
  Bosun's docker-executor subject, with Tailscale Funnel available for
  showing showcases to interested parties on request. It just stops being
  the *public* demoability path.
- **Three classes of exhibit:** static → stays live on Cloudflare Pages;
  backend-dependent → video; the fleet → test bed. Recorded in the root
  `CLAUDE.md`.
- **The L-system goes.** Not worth reviving: the hylograph-drawn zoo was
  abandoned because matching the quality of Claude's own drawings was an
  unreasonable amount of work.
- **`docs/` should move up out of the site repo.** Agreed in principle;
  shape still being settled (see Next Session).
